Purpose

SafeCity is a civic issue reporting prototype. Reports are stored in the submitting browser and are not automatically sent to government departments or emergency services.

No emergency service

Do not use this website to request urgent emergency assistance. Contact the appropriate emergency service directly when immediate assistance is needed.

Responsible submissions

Provide honest descriptions and lawful content. Do not submit knowingly false, threatening, abusive or harassing reports.

Photographs and personal information

Only submit photographs you have permission to use. Avoid exposing identifiable people, private documents or sensitive personal details.

Accuracy and status

Addresses, descriptions and coordinates may be inaccurate. A report marked resolved is a local user-controlled status, not independent verification that a real-world issue has been fixed.

Third-party services

Mapping, geocoding and font services are supplied by third parties and may be unavailable or subject to their own terms.

Operator information

Before public launch, provide the operator's legal name and required contact details, and obtain legal review of these terms.
