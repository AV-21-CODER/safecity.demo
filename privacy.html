SITE POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This is starter wording for a prototype, not legal advice. The site operator must review and update it before public launch.

Information stored

This prototype stores report category, description, location text, coordinates, priority, optional name, status and submission time in local browser storage. Reports are not uploaded to a SafeCity server.

Photographs

Selected photographs are previewed in the browser. This version does not upload them to a server or attach them permanently to a report.

Location information

If you grant browser location permission, coordinates may be sent to OpenStreetMap's public Nominatim service to obtain a readable address. You can type a location instead.

Third-party services

The map uses Leaflet and OpenStreetMap map tiles. Address lookup uses Nominatim. Fonts may be requested from Google Fonts. These providers may receive technical information when your browser requests resources.

Retention and security

Reports remain in the browser profile until site data is removed or overwritten. Anyone with access to that profile may be able to view them. This prototype does not provide server-side access controls.

Contact

Before launch, add the operator's legal name and a monitored privacy contact address. Update this policy to reflect the actual deployed service and obtain appropriate legal review.
